Flexibility & Safety

Essential Warm-Ups & Stretches

Dynamic warm-ups before training prevent injury; static stretches after increase range of motion.

01

Leg Swings (Front & Lateral)

Swing leg smoothly forward and backward, then side-to-side to open hamstrings and hip adductors.

15 swings per leg
02

Hip Circles & Torso Rotations

Rotate hips in wide circles, followed by dynamic torso twists to prepare spine for spinning kicks.

10 rotations each direction
03

High Knee & Butt Kick Drills

Light jogging raising knees to chest level, then kicking heels to glutes to raise body temperature.

30 seconds continuous
01

Butterfly Stretch

Sit with soles of feet together, gently press knees down toward the floor to target inner thighs/groin.

Hold 45-60 seconds
02

Seated Straddle Split

Spread legs wide in V-position, lean forward from hips maintaining straight spine for kicking height.

Hold 60 seconds
03

Runner's Lunge Hip Flexor Stretch

Lunge forward with back knee on floor, push hips down and forward to release tight hip flexors.

Hold 30s per side

Rest & Regeneration

Post-Training & Competition Recovery

Optimal recovery routines ensure long-term performance and injury prevention.

1. Rehydration & Electrolytes

Replenish fluids immediately after intense sessions. Drink water mixed with electrolytes to prevent muscle cramps.

2. Protein & Carbohydrate Intake

Consume a balance of protein (muscle repair) and carbs (glycogen store restoration) within 45 minutes after training.

3. Sleep Quality (8-9 Hours)

Sleep is when deep muscle tissue repair occurs. Crucial for teenagers balancing academics and martial arts.

4. Cryotherapy / Cold Compress

Apply ice packs to bruised or inflamed joints after hard sparring sessions to reduce swelling and speed recovery.
