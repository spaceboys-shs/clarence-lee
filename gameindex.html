The Neuroscience of Human Reflexes

How your brain pathways process sensory cues and convert them into muscular action.

1. The Neural Pathway (What happens in ~200 milliseconds)

  1. Sensory Perception (~20-40ms): Photons strike retina, creating electrical impulses down optic nerve.
  2. Brain Processing (~60-100ms): Signal reaches visual cortex, moving to motor cortex to decide action.
  3. Motor Signal Transmission (~30-50ms): Signal travels down spinal cord to finger muscles.
  4. Muscle Activation (~20-30ms): Finger muscles contract and press mouse button.
